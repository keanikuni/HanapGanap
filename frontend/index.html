our team

Introducing the HanapGanap team: a group of students from Pampanga State University who made the website for their course Web Systems and Technologies 313 or WEBSYS 313. The team consists of 2 main groups led by Jhorelle Tiongco, the backend team: Dave Andrew Quintana, Christian Ocampo, Kurt Lawrence Cervantes, and Renan Joseph Capili, and the frontend team: Georgina Mikaela Macaspac, Mica Jane Jose, Ricarlo Pineda, and Gabriel Cyrist Lacsina, wherein Tiongco also worked in. We hope you enjoy your time at HanapGanap, and your next event be filled with joy! 🌸
